Both the baseline and the planning-only condition agents did not correctly interpret "right wall" relative to how a user would look at the front of the house (where the inital doors and windows are).
By contrast, the conditions with sketching correctly positioned the windows and did not need to specify their location/number through the text prompt at all.

The planning-only condition agent created a wall_thickness parameter for hollowing out the house and a separate step for adding side windows with offset parameters, reusing the window_width and window_height parameters from the previous "Add Windows" step.
The full condition created new parameters for the side windows. This may simply be an artifact of how similar my window sketches were to the existing windows.

Intrestingly, the conditions without planning hollowed out the roof in addition to the main house body. They also did not care to keep a floor. The conditions with planning did not hollow out the roof, but kept a floor when hollowing out the main house body.
